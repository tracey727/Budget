# Industry Pack Roadmap

The core platform stays the same. Industry packs change mappings, vocabulary and deterministic rules.

## Pack 1 — Psychology / Allied Health / NDIS
First release.
Primary opportunities: cancelled slots, no-shows, unbilled completed services, overdue invoices, referrals, waitlists and overdue operational follow-up.

## Pack 2 — Dental
Potential later rules: cancelled chair time, treatment-plan follow-up, unbilled completed work, overdue account actions and recall gaps. Domain/legal review required before release.

## Pack 3 — Pharmacy Operations
Potential later rules: unresolved operational follow-ups, stock/ordering discrepancies and workflow exceptions. ScriptGuard remains a separate specialised product; do not merge dispensing/prescribing logic into Revenue Rescue.

## Pack 4 — Service Stations / Retail
Potential later rules: supplier discrepancies, missed credits, inventory exceptions, expiry/shrinkage signals and unresolved operational tasks. Stock Sense remains separately governed and may integrate later rather than being copied into V1.

## Pack 5 — Trades / Field Services
Potential later rules: completed jobs not invoiced, quoted work not followed up, deposits/milestones unmatched and overdue accounts.

## Rule for expansion
Do not build another pack until the first paid/pilot market produces enough evidence to justify expansion.
